Good for
  • The most familiar pattern (GitHub, Linear, Jira): counts sit where you act on them.
  • Takes about 50px instead of 110px of the screen's best space.
  • Scales: a fifth filter later is one more tab.
Watch out
  • Rows hidden by a filter are out of sight; "All" must stay the default.
  • The coral count is small; the coral "Update all" button carries the urgency.
